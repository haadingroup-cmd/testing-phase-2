## Har month ka simple routine (30 min/week)
1. Hafte mein 2–3 nayi directory/profile submit karo (Tier 1 se shuru).
2. Har complete client se **ek genuine review** maango (Clutch/Google/Trustpilot).
3. Ek LinkedIn post + ek blog post publish karo.

## Golden rules
- **Kabhi backlinks kharido mat** (Fiverr "1000 backlinks" wale) — Google penalize karta hai.
- Har jagah **bilkul same** naam/phone/address.
- Quality > quantity. 10 high-authority links > 500 spammy links.
- Reviews maango — ye ranking + trust dono barhati hain.
