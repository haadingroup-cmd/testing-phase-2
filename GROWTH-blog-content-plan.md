# HaadinGlobal — Blog Content Plan (Organic Traffic Engine)

Abhi blog ke posts placeholder hain (ek hi generic paragraph). Real, helpful articles
likhne se **international organic traffic** aati hai — kyunki ye searches location par
depend nahi karti. Neeche 12 article ideas hain, search intent ke hisaab se, jo aapke
target markets (Pakistan + Gulf + UK/US) mein log actually search karte hain.

**Rule:** Har article 1,000–1,500+ words, real value, apne kaam ke examples/screenshots,
aur end par ek CTA ("Book a free consultation"). AI se draft karwa sakte ho lekin apni
awaaz aur real examples zaroor daalo — Google "helpful content" ko reward karta hai.

---

## Batch 1 — Cost / pricing (high buyer intent, easy wins)
1. **"How Much Does Meta Ads Management Cost in 2026?"**
   Keyword: meta ads management cost / facebook ads agency pricing.
2. **"Google Ads vs Meta Ads: Which Is Better for Your Business?"**
   Comparison table = featured snippet chance.
3. **"How Much Should a Small Business Spend on Digital Marketing?"**

## Batch 2 — Gulf / location intent (feeds your Ads landing pages)

5. **"How to Market Your Business in Saudi Arabia: A Practical Guide"**
6. **"Shopify vs WooCommerce for Gulf eCommerce Stores"**

## Batch 3 — How-to / informational (top-of-funnel traffic)

8. **"Local SEO Checklist: Rank on Google Maps in 30 Days"**
9. **"7 Reasons Your Facebook Ads Aren't Converting (and How to Fix Them)"**

## Batch 4 — Trust / case-study style (converts readers to leads)
11. **"Case Study: How We Cut a Client's Cost-Per-Lead by 60%"**
    (Use a real, verifiable result — no fake numbers.)
12. **"What to Look for When Hiring a Digital Marketing Agency"**

---

## Publishing rhythm
- Start: **1 article per week** (Batch 1 first — closest to buying).
- Each article: internal-link to a relevant service page + one Gulf landing page.
- After publishing: submit URL in Google Search Console → Request Indexing.
- Share each on LinkedIn + Instagram + Facebook.

## SEO checklist per article
- [ ] Target keyword in title, first paragraph, one H2, and URL slug.
- [ ] One clear H1, logical H2/H3 structure.
- [ ] Meta title (<60 chars) + meta description (<155 chars).
- [ ] At least 2 internal links + 1 external link to an authority source.
- [ ] One image with descriptive alt text.
- [ ] FAQ block at the end (feeds AI Overviews / rich results).
- [ ] CTA to /consultation or WhatsApp.

> Tip: The blog data lives in `src/data/blog.ts`. Replace the placeholder body
> text there with the real article content as you write each one.
